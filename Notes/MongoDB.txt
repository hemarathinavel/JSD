History of Mongodb
===================
Mongo was created by Eliot and Dwight n 2007,
when they faced scalability issues
 while working wht relational database.
The orgsnization that developed MongoDB was orginally known as 10gen.
In Feb 2009,they changed their bussiness model and released Mongodb as an open source project.
The organizaton changed its name in 2013 and now known as MangoDB Inc.

Features of Mongodb
===================
1) Mongodb provides high performance.
Most of the operations n the mongodb are faster compared to relational databases.
2)mongodb provides auto replicatoion feature that allows you to quickly recover data in case of a failure.
3) Horizontal scaling is possible in mongodb because of sharing.sharding is partitioning of data and placing it on multiple machines in such a way that the order of the data is preserved
