Self-Introduction Pitch Points -

1. Name & Educational Background

2. Internship / Work Experience (if any)

3. Relevant Skills & Technical Expertise

4. Key Projects / Practical Experience

5. Achievements / Certifications

6. Strengths

7. Career Goal

8. Why I Am a Good Fit to Work as a Software Developer

9. Closing Statement
 
 
Examples of Closing Statements -

1. I am looking forward to starting my career as a Software Developer, where I can apply my technical skills, learn from experienced professionals, and contribute to the organization’s success.”

2. As a fresher, I am eager to learn, take on new challenges, and continuously improve my technical skills while contributing effectively as a Software Developer.”

3. I believe my technical knowledge, willingness to learn, and problem-solving skills will help me become a valuable Software Developer and contribute positively to the team.
